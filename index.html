AI where it helps.
Determinism where it matters.

The system separates document understanding from financial validation. Claude interprets the visual document; ordinary code verifies arithmetic and controls what can enter the final output.

01 · VISION

Read scanned payslips

Pages are rasterized and sent through a structured Claude Vision extraction flow. Unclear values are returned as null rather than invented.

02 · LAYOUT

Learn the office format

A reference salary or tax statement can be analyzed into a reusable layout specification for the generic document renderer.

03 · VERIFY

Block bad arithmetic

Extracted earnings and deductions are recomputed against printed totals. Mismatches become a hard review gate, not a silent warning.

Built around “blank over guess.”

Financial documents are the wrong place for confident-looking hallucinations. VetanIQ’s implementation is designed to prefer missing data over fabricated certainty.

No guessed numbers

Fields that cannot be read clearly remain empty. Missing months remain blank, and downstream tax-liability lines can stay blocked until the required months are confirmed.

Human confirmation

Extracted monthly values and inferred employer layouts are reviewed before they are trusted as inputs to generated documents.

Versioned templates

Employer layouts and field mappings are represented as data instead of being buried in one-off rendering code.

Fiscal-year rules

Tax rules are loaded by financial year, keeping calculation logic separate from document rendering.

Building the safer document pipeline.
